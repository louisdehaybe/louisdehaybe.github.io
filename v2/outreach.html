Outreach

Sharing robotics beyond the lab: workshops for school students, demonstrations and talks, teaching and academic service.

2020 to 2025

Robotics workshops for school students

As robotics instructor, I participated to the organization of several hands on workshops with ABB RobotStudio and programming competitions on the Sawyer robot for bachelor and visiting secondary school students.

  • Athénée Royal M. Destenay, February 2025. Sawyer programming competition with second year secondary school students.
  • Secondary Education Day, January 2024 and 2025. Sawyer programming competition for last year secondary school students visiting the university to explore their options for further studies.
  • "Program your robot", 17 May 2023. Sawyer programming competition organized with Réjouisciences, for students in the 4th to 6th year of secondary school.
  • Printemps des Sciences, April 2022 and March 2023. Sawyer programming competition for visiting secondary school students.
  • Robotix Academy, March 2022. Sawyer programming competition.
